# UrGlowUp Product

## Register

product

## Users

Turkey-based customers looking for beauty, grooming, wellness, spa, and personal-care services, together with the businesses and administrators who manage those services. Customers need to compare trustworthy business information and book quickly, primarily from mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

UrGlowUp makes local personal-care businesses discoverable and turns their public profiles into a clear path from evaluation to appointment. Success means customers can understand services, prices, availability, location, and reputation without digging, while businesses can manage the same information from a focused operational interface.

## Brand Personality

Warm, premium, and trustworthy. The product should feel calm and approachable while remaining direct enough for task-focused booking and business workflows.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic lifestyle aspiration, beauty-industry cliches, visually noisy marketplaces, dark patterns, decorative motion, and interfaces that hide price, duration, availability, or cancellation details. Fresha may inform the clarity of the business-profile structure, but UrGlowUp should not imitate it visually.

## Design Principles

1. Trust first: make prices, duration, availability, policies, and reputation easy to verify.
2. Calm focus: remove visual noise and keep the next useful action obvious.
3. Mobile first: prioritize one-thumb use and fast public-profile loading.
4. Marketplace clarity: keep core business and booking information consistently visible.
5. Shared source of truth: public profiles, marketplace discovery, and business management should present the same underlying information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum. Preserve visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, text labels for icon-only controls, reduced-motion preferences, and non-color indicators for status. Keep the visual language inclusive across beauty, grooming, clinic, and wellness services.
